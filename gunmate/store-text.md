# Gunmate: store text

**Description (about 150 characters)**
Real chess where every capture becomes a first-person gunfight on the board. Pick your piece's gun, win the duel, take the square. Play friends online.

**Objective (about 150 characters)**
Checkmate the enemy king. To capture, your piece must win a first-person duel against the defender; lose and your attacker falls instead.

**Controls**
- Board: click a piece, then a highlighted square. Drag to turn the board, scroll to zoom.
- Duel: WASD move, mouse aims, hold left click to fire, Space jumps (knights double jump), right click scopes (bishop).
