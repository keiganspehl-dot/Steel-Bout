      How to Fight

      Move the mouseThou seest through thine own eyes, and thy sword hand followeth the mouse. Sweep it hard across the screen and through thy foe. Speed and weight are what wound.
      W A S D / arrowsStep in, fall back and circle. Thou always facest thy foe. Keep a little distance, so the blade landeth at full speed.
      Right-click / SpaceHold it for the thrusting grip: point straight along thine arm, then drive the tip forward. A good thrust sticketh fast: keep holding and move the mouse to haul thy foe about, then let go to pull the blade free.
      C / CtrlDrop low.
      E / TabWhen several stand against thee, turn to face the next.
      EscFree the mouse and pause (in a fight with friends, the fight goeth on without thee). Click the field to take up thy sword again.

      A blow to the head woundeth worst. A swift cut from sword or axe may take off an arm or a leg, and whoso loseth his sword arm or a leg must yield. Cuts glance off good armour, so bring a mace or poleaxe against a knight. Put thy blade in the way to parry.

      On a touch screen: drag anywhere to move thy hand. The arrows move thee, and the buttons drop low and thrust.
